Project Calyx: Exploring Local AI and Secure Systems

Artificial intelligence is becoming part of more software systems, but building useful AI tools is not only about choosing a powerful model. It is also about how models work together, how information is handled, and how these systems remain under the user's control.

Project Calyx is an ongoing project by Oblivion focused on exploring local AI systems, model collaboration, and the security of connected AI tools.

The project is connected to our work on Angra and Nythos. Angra explores controlled collaboration between local models in environments such as LM Studio and Bionic. Nythos focuses on the development of a local AI assistant, including ideas around persistent memory and user control over stored information.

Write on Medium
One of our interests is understanding how these tools could work together more effectively without making local systems unnecessarily complex or resource-intensive.

We are also exploring the relationship between local AI development and cybersecurity. As AI tools become more connected to other applications, devices, and online services, questions about permissions, data access, identity, and secure communication become increasingly important.

For Calyx, these are areas of research and development, not claims that every planned capability is already implemented. Our goal is to understand the technical challenges, test practical approaches, and gradually develop a clearer architecture for local AI systems and their secure interaction.

Calyx is still a work in progress. We are sharing our research as it develops, learning from existing tools and standards, and documenting what we build along the way.

Project Calyx is part of our ongoing effort to build practical AI tools while keeping security, transparency, and user control in mind.
